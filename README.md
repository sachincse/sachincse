I'm a **Senior ML Engineer with 8 years** of experience taking AI from notebook to production across **banking/fintech and healthcare**. My recent work sits at the intersection of **agentic developer tooling on the Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, **graph machine learning & knowledge graphs**, and the **MLOps** that keeps models reliable — CI/CD, containerized serving, drift monitoring, and incident response.

I'm equally at home in **Generative AI** (production RAG, LLM evaluation, multi-agent tooling) and in the plumbing that decides whether a model actually survives contact with real traffic. I like explaining complex systems clearly — to engineers and to the people who don't write code.

- 🔭 Building **agentic developer tooling** and **knowledge-graph-backed ML** in production
